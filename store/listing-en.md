## Detailed description

```
Free games on Epic Games Store, GOG and Amazon Luna appear on a schedule, stay
up for a few days, and are gone. Missing one costs nothing but happens every
time you are away for a week.

This extension watches for them and claims them for you.

WHAT IT DOES

- Checks Epic Games Store, GOG, Amazon Luna and Steam for games that are free
  to keep, not merely free to play for a weekend.
- Claims them on its own, in a small window that closes by itself. You can
  turn this off and keep notifications only.
- Collects Amazon Luna redemption codes and tells you how many days each one
  has left before it expires.
- Gathers what Steam, GOG and Epic say you already own into a single page, so
  you stop opening a store page for a game you have had for years.
- Learns when each store tends to rotate its offers, and looks more often
  around those hours instead of polling all day.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

- No account, no sign-up, no server of ours. Everything stays in your browser.
- No telemetry. No install identifier, no crash reports, no usage statistics.
- It never reads your library on its own initiative. That happens on a click,
  and only towards the store concerned. Only the sign-in check runs by itself
  (adjustable): it keeps nothing but "signed in / not".
- It never works around a captcha or a rate limit. A security challenge hands
  control back to you and raises the tab.
- Steam claiming is off by default and stays off until you turn it on, because
  the Steam Subscriber Agreement forbids scripted interaction. The extension
  shows you the offer and puts it one click away. The click is yours.

PRIVACY

Everything lives in your browser's local storage: the offer catalogue, your
settings, your history, your pending codes. Nothing is encrypted by the
extension and nothing is sent to us, because there is no us to send it to.

One setting can copy pending codes into your browser's synced storage. It is
off by default, and it is the only thing that can ever leave the machine.

OPEN SOURCE

The engine is generated from 34 readable modules, and the build is verified
byte for byte on every commit. The published archives are reproducible: build
the same tag and you get the same SHA256.
```
